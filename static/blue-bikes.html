Campus Rules

  • Maximum speed 15 mph on campus roads
  • No riding on sidewalks or inside buildings
  • Park only in designated scooter zones — do not block walkways
  • Helmet strongly recommended
  • Must be 18+ and have a valid driver's license

Tips for Commuters

Getting to Campus
  • JFK/UMass Station is the closest MBTA stop — only a 5-minute bike ride from the main campus buildings.
  • Lock bikes at the designated bike racks near Campus Center and Healey Library — do not lock to handrails or fences.
  • Combine Bluebikes with the Red Line for a faster commute from downtown Boston.
  • Check the Bluebikes app before leaving — station availability varies during peak hours (8–10 AM, 4–6 PM).
  • The UMass Shuttle is free with your UMass ID and runs continuously between JFK/UMass Station and campus — a reliable backup.
