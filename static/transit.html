Commuter Tips

Getting Here Faster
  • Best combo: Red Line to JFK/UMass, then free UMass Shuttle to Campus Center — about 25 min from Downtown Crossing.
  • Monthly LinkPass ($90) covers all subway and local bus rides — more economical if you commute 3+ days/week.
  • Avoid peak hours (8–9 AM inbound, 5–6 PM outbound) if possible — trains run every 4–6 min but can be crowded.
